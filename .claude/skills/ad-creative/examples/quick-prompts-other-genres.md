# Quick prompts in other genres

Each example is one clip delivered the quick way: its keyframe prompt first,
then the video prompt that animates it, a negative and one line of settings.
The numbering runs across examples so the linter pairs each clip with its
keyframe.

### Keyframe 1 · anime opening · Nano Banana Pro
**References:** none (character from the description) · **Save as:** keyframe_c1.png
```
Japanese-style anime still, cel-shaded characters with clean line art, detailed hand-painted backgrounds, dramatic lighting, dynamic camera angles, expressive eyes, wind in hair and clothes.
```
