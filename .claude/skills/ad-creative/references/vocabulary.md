# Vocabulary

Prompt words in English, with a Vietnamese gloss for explaining choices to the
user. Pick one or two words per slot. More than about eight technical terms in a
prompt makes it worse, not better.

## Shot size (cỡ cảnh)
| term | gloss / use |
| --- | --- |
| extreme wide / establishing shot | toàn cảnh, mở cảnh, chủ thể rất nhỏ |
| wide / full body shot | thấy toàn thân và bối cảnh |
| medium shot | từ thắt lưng lên, nói chuyện |
| medium close-up | từ vai lên, biểu cảm |
| close-up | mặt hoặc sản phẩm chiếm khung |
| extreme close-up / macro | chi tiết: mắt, kết cấu, giọt nước |
| insert shot | cận một chi tiết hành động (tay bấm nút) |

## Lens and texture (ống kính, chất hình)
24mm (rộng) · 35mm (kể chuyện) · 50mm (tự nhiên) · 85mm (chân dung) · macro
lens · shallow depth of field, f/1.8 · deep focus · anamorphic flare · motion
blur 180-degree shutter · 35mm film grain · halation · soft diffusion filter ·
phone lens look · 60fps slow motion.

## Colour (màu)
warm amber palette · cool blue palette · teal and orange · muted / desaturated ·
vibrant saturated · pastel · monochrome with one accent colour · earth tones ·
jewel tones · cream and brown · red and gold festive · lifted blacks (phim mờ) ·
no color grading (UGC).

## Mood (cảm xúc) - one or two
cozy · calm · joyful · playful · intimate · nostalgic · dreamy · mysterious ·
tense · eerie · dramatic · triumphant · majestic · melancholic · urgent ·
confident · deadpan.

## Weather and atmosphere (thời tiết)
light snowfall · frost on glass · fog / ground mist · drizzle, wet asphalt
reflections · heavy rain on the window · steam rising · dust in a light beam ·
heat haze · starry night · stormy sky.

## Materials (chất liệu) - for products
matte · glossy · brushed metal · chrome · frosted glass · clear glass with
condensation · ceramic glaze · knit wool · fleece · velvet · worn leather ·
polished wood grain · marble · silicone · soft-touch plastic · iridescent.
