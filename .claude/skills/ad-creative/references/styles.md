# Style blocks

Each style is a block of fixed words for the STYLE lock. Paste it word for word
into every clip; add location, time of day, light direction, palette and ratio
after it. Style blocks carry no camera move: the move belongs to each clip, one
per clip. "Moves that suit" lines are suggestions for the clips.

You may trim a block once, when you build the lock (for example to 4-6 UGC
anchors). After that the trimmed lock is fixed and pasted word for word. Show the user the short menu (name + one line) and let them pick by
number, or describe their own.

The blocks describe looks instead of naming studios or artists, because models
follow a described look more reliably and some refuse named IP. If the user
wants a named look, a franchise or a character, write it as asked.

Vietnamese labels are for talking to the user; the blocks stay in English.

## A. Live action

### 1. Cinematic realism (điện ảnh, như quay bằng máy cinema)
For: brand films, short films, premium ads, hero shots.
```
Photorealistic live-action footage shot on a cinema camera, 35mm prime lens,
natural skin texture with visible pores, real fabric wrinkles, natural motion
blur, soft motivated lighting, subtle film grain, true-to-life colour, shallow
depth of field
```
Negative: `plastic skin, waxy face, CGI look, oversaturated, extra fingers, flicker`

### 2. UGC phone footage (người dùng tự quay bằng điện thoại)
For: reviews, unboxing, "TikTok made me buy it", testimonials. Best performer for
social product ads.
```
Vertical smartphone footage, handheld with slight micro-shake, natural daylight
from a window, no ring light, real skin tones, no color grading, no beauty
filter, auto-exposure feel, ordinary lived-in room, casual and unpolished
```
Negative: `cinematic grading, studio lighting, ring light, gift box, logo, subtitles`
Tip: when you build the lock you may trim it to 4-6 anchors; keep the light and
grade anchors, drop extra camera anchors first. The short bans in this block (no
ring light, no color grading, no beauty filter) are a deliberate exception to
"describe what you want": they are the proven levers against the glossy AI look.
Vary background props across clips; props are not part of the lock.

### 3. Documentary / vlog (tài liệu, vlog)
```
Documentary-style handheld footage, available natural light, candid unposed
moments, real locations, observational camera, slight lens flare, natural sound
```

### 4. Luxury commercial (quảng cáo cao cấp)
For: jewellery, perfume, watches, premium tech, spirits.
```
High-end luxury commercial, controlled studio lighting with soft rim light,
deep rich blacks, glossy controlled reflections, slow-motion macro details,
slow elegant camera moves, refined premium colour grade
```
Negative: `cheap plastic look, clutter, harsh flash, fingerprints, oversaturated`

### 5. Tabletop product B-roll (sản phẩm trên bàn, không người)
For: food, cosmetics, gadgets, any product without a person.
```
Studio tabletop product footage, macro lens, clean seamless background, crisp
specular highlights, soft shadow under the product, slow-motion particles or
splashes where they fit
```
Moves that suit: slow orbit, slow push in, top-down static.

### 6. Cozy hygge home (ấm cúng, nhà mùa đông)
For: winter, holiday, candles, blankets, warm drinks, home goods.
```
Warm cozy home footage, tungsten lamp light and candle glow, soft blankets and
knit textures, steam from mugs, rain or snow visible through the window, amber
and cream palette, gentle slow camera, intimate quiet mood
```

### 7. Festive holiday (không khí lễ hội, Giáng sinh)
```
Festive winter holiday footage, warm string lights with soft bokeh, evergreen
branches, red and gold accents, wrapping paper and ribbon textures, cold blue
dusk outside the window against warm interior light, joyful gentle mood
```

### 8. Bright clean lifestyle (tươi sáng, sạch sẽ)
For: wellness, kitchen, cleaning, organisation, kids products.
```
Bright airy lifestyle footage, high-key natural daylight, white and soft pastel
surfaces, clean uncluttered rooms, gentle gimbal movement, fresh optimistic mood
```

### 9. Gritty street (đường phố, gai góc)
```
Gritty urban handheld footage, hard midday light or sodium street lamps at
night, wet asphalt reflections, high contrast, desaturated greens, 16mm grain,
raw energy
```

### 10. Neon noir night (đêm neon)
```
Night city footage, neon signs in cyan and magenta, wet reflective streets,
rim light from shop windows, deep shadows, light haze, moody and mysterious
```
Moves that suit: slow side tracking, static wide.

### 11. Vintage film (phim cũ, hoài niệm)
```
Vintage 1990s film look, soft film grain, slightly faded warm colours, gentle
halation around highlights, mild vignette, 24fps cadence, nostalgic mood
```
Variants: `VHS camcorder footage, scan lines, colour bleed, date stamp in post`;
`Super 8 home movie, heavy grain, light leaks, flicker`.

### 12. Horror / thriller (kinh dị, giật gân)
```
Low-key horror cinematography, single practical light source, deep crushed
shadows, cold desaturated palette, thin ground fog, unsettling stillness, sharp
sound design
```
Moves that suit: slow creeping push in, static with something moving in frame.

### 13. Epic nature / travel (phong cảnh hùng vĩ, du lịch)
```
Grand-scale landscape footage, golden hour or blue hour, volumetric light
through mist, vast scale with small human figure, rich natural colour,
awe-inspiring mood
```
Moves that suit: aerial drone glide, slow crane up, pull back to reveal.

### 14. Sports / action (thể thao, hành động)
```
High-energy action footage, hard directional light, high contrast, motion blur
at 180-degree shutter, low angles, sweat and dust detail, intense mood
```
Moves that suit: tracking, whip pan between clips, low-angle static.

```
Futuristic sci-fi city, holographic UI elements, cyan and magenta neon, wet
```

```
Large-scale cinematic fantasy, grand scale architecture, volumetric god rays, glowing
```


### 28. Custom
Turn the user's description or reference image into a block in the same shape:
medium, light, lens or rendering, palette, motion, mood. Read it back and get a
yes before using it.

## Consistency tips by family

- **Live action:** lock face details, outfit colours, lens, light direction and
  time of day. Use a real product photo as the first frame.
- **Animated:** generate a character model sheet in the style first and use it
  as the reference for every clip. Animated styles drift more, so repeat the
  whole block every time and add `NOT photorealistic, NOT live action` to the
  negative.
- **Motion graphics:** lock hex colours and layout grid; all text in the editor.
- One style per video, unless the user picked 27.
- Style words must agree: not "anime" with "photorealistic", not "golden hour"
  with "heavy downpour".
